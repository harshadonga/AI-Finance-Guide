{{i:warning}} Limits of this dashboard

It uses the simplified methods of Module 0: teaching heuristics for the emergency fund, a needs-based life-cover estimate at a 2% real return, pre-tax goal returns, and no tax detail. It is a thinking tool for your own decisions, not professional financial advice.

Rubric

  • {{i:check}} Complete chain: every section filled with your real numbers, or deliberately marked zero.
  • {{i:check}} Honest valuation: assets at realisable value (Lesson 0.3), essentials separated from choices (Lesson 0.2).
  • {{i:check}} Diagnosis: names the one or two biggest fragilities, using the dashboard's numbers as evidence.
  • {{i:check}} Actions: three, in priority order, each with a reason tied to a lesson (for example, "clear the card first: 51% effective cost, Lesson 0.8").
  • {{i:check}} Review date: when you will update it (every 3 to 6 months, and after any major change).
