{{i:warning}} Constructed answer to audit

“When you buy a share, the company receives your money immediately. A market order guarantees the displayed quote, and the broker stores the shares until the trade settles on the next calendar day. Stock splits increase value because investors own more shares.”

Every sentence is wrong or incomplete. A sound correction distinguishes primary from secondary transactions, quotes from fills, brokers from depositories, working days from calendar days and units from economic value.
