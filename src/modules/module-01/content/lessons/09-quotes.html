Bid, Ask and Spread

A quote is two prices plus quantities. Trading cost depends on how much depth your order consumes.

Read both sides

The best bid is the highest displayed price a buyer is currently offering. The best ask is the lowest displayed price a seller is currently offering. Their difference is the spread. The midpoint is a reference, not a guaranteed trade price.

{{i:function}} Formula

Spread=Best ask−Best bid

If bid = ₹99.90 and ask = ₹100.10, the spread is ₹0.20. Relative to the ₹100 midpoint, that is 0.20%.

Liquidity has several dimensions

  • Tightness: how narrow is the spread?
  • Depth: how much quantity is available near the best price?
  • Resilience: how quickly does the book recover after a large trade?
  • Immediacy: how quickly can the desired quantity trade?

A high daily volume does not guarantee deep liquidity at the moment you trade. Displayed orders can also be cancelled, so visible depth is evidence, not a promise.
