Order Types

An order is a set of instructions. Every instruction trades one form of control for another.

Orders also compete

In a common price-time priority system, a better price ranks ahead of a worse price; at the same price, the earlier eligible order ranks ahead. Seeing a quantity displayed does not mean your later order will be first to receive it.

{{i:prohibit}} Common misconception

“A stop-loss guarantees my exit price.”

Why this failsA stop normally controls when another order is released. A market stop can fill far away after a gap. A stop-limit can protect price and remain unfilled.

Better questionWhich risk matters more here: a poor price or no execution?
