Depositories, Clearing and Settlement

Settlement cycles are calendar rules

T is the trade day. T+1 is the next eligible working day, not necessarily the next calendar day. Indian cash-equity trades on NSE generally settle on T+1, while an optional T+0 segment exists for eligible securities and trades. Always check the applicable segment and current exchange rule.

{{i:seal-check}} Current rule verified 30 Sep 2026 from NSE Clearing and Settlement (opens in a new tab) and NSE Market Segments (opens in a new tab).

What can fail

  • An order can be rejected before matching.
  • A trade can exist while funds or securities are not yet settled.
  • A seller can fail to deliver, triggering shortage procedures under market rules.
  • A broker statement can disagree with the depository record and must be investigated.

{{i:warning}} Safety

Do not treat unsettled proceeds or an app's provisional display as the same thing as completed, withdrawable ownership.
