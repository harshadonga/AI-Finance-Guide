1.6

Brokers

A broker gives a client access to an exchange and routes orders; the broker is not the exchange, the depository or the investment decision.

Three accounts, three jobs

Bank accountMoves funds
Trading accountSubmits and records orders through a broker
[[demat|Demat account]]Holds securities through a depository participant

A provider may offer all three through one interface. The legal functions remain distinct. This distinction matters when a platform fails, when reconciling statements and when deciding where an asset is actually recorded.

Discount and full-service models

A discount broker typically emphasises execution and digital tools with a narrower service bundle. A full-service broker may add research, advisory, relationship support or other services. The label does not establish safety or suitability. Registration, controls, service terms and total costs matter more.

{{i:calculator}} Cost stack

{{tag:hyp}}

A ₹1,00,000 order may involve brokerage, exchange charges, taxes, regulatory fees and the bid-ask spread. A zero-brokerage claim does not make the total trading cost zero. Compare a complete contract note and the executed price, not one advertised fee.

Verify before funding

  1. Search the legal entity and registration number on SEBI's recognised-intermediary register.
  2. Confirm exchange membership and the authorised application or domain.
  3. Read the tariff sheet, client agreement and complaint route.
  4. Reconcile broker records with depository statements.
  5. Never share passwords, PINs, OTPs or remote access.

{{i:seal-check}} Verification route checked 30 Sep 2026: SEBI Recognised Intermediaries (opens in a new tab). SEBI states that investors can verify broker registration on SEBI and exchange websites.

Common misconception

{{i:prohibit}} Common misconception

“The broker holds my shares, so a broker app balance is enough.”

Why this failsDematerialised holdings are recorded through a depository participant in the depository system. Broker and depository records should reconcile, but they are not the same record.

Better questionWhat does my depository statement show, and does it match the broker statement?
